# KDCN Brand Usage Guidelines

**KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK (KDCN)**

---

## 1. Brand Overview

KDCN stands for **KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK** — a Kenya-based digital systems organization headquartered in Manyatta, Embu, Kenya, with a global technology outlook.

### Brand Positioning

> *"No data left unguarded. No system left unsafe."*

### Brand Promise

To design, develop, and support secure, resilient, scalable, and sustainable digital systems.

---

## 2. Logo Usage

### Primary Logo

The official KDCN logo consists of:
- **The Wolf** — symbolizing vigilance, guardianship, and strength.
- **The Monitor** — representing technology, systems, and digital infrastructure.
- **The Name** — "KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK" or "KDCN"

### Logo Variations

| Variation | When to Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Full Logo** | Primary use — homepage, headers, official documents |
| **Short Logo (KDCN)** | Secondary use — social media, favicon, small spaces |
| **Icon Only** | Mobile app icon, favicon, watermark |

### Logo Colors

The KDCN logo uses the following colors:

| Color | Hex Code | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Blue** | `#0b7fab` | Main brand color |
| **Accent Cyan** | `#00d1ff` | Highlights and accents |
| **White** | `#ffffff` | On dark backgrounds |
| **Black/Dark** | `#0a0f14` | On light backgrounds |

### Logo Do's and Don'ts

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
| :--- | :--- |
| Use the logo as provided | Stretch or distort the logo |
| Maintain clear space around the logo | Change the logo colors |
| Use high-resolution versions | Place the logo on busy backgrounds |
| Use the logo in official communications | Rotate or flip the logo |

## 5. Brand Voice and Tone

### Voice

- **Professional** — Speaks with authority and expertise.
- **Confident** — Clear, decisive, and trustworthy.
- **Security-Focused** — Emphasizes protection, safety, and discipline.
- **Forward-Thinking** — Innovative, modern, and future-oriented.

### Tone

- **Formal but Approachable** — Professional without being cold.
- **Clear and Concise** — No unnecessary jargon.
- **Reassuring** — Builds trust through transparency.

### Tagline

> *"No data left unguarded. No system left unsafe."*

### Motto

> *"KDCN — The Digital System."*
> *"Built in Manyatta. Engineered for the world."*

## 6. Imagery and Visual Style

### Photography Style

- **Clean and Modern** — High-quality, professional images.
- **Technology-Focused** — Cybersecurity, systems, infrastructure, cloud.
- **Human-Centric** — People using technology securely.
- **Dark and Moody** — For security-related imagery.

### Icon Style

- **Minimalist** — Simple, clean, line-based icons.
- **Consistent** — Same style and thickness across all icons.
- **Color** — Primary Blue or Accent Cyan on dark backgrounds.

## 7. Web and Digital Usage

### Website Guidelines

- **Dark Mode** — Default for KDCN website.
- **Light Mode** — Optional for accessibility.
- **Cyan Highlights** — Use Accent Cyan for CTAs and links.
