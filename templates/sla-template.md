# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK (KDCN)**

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") is entered into between **KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK (KDCN)** ("Provider") and the client ("Client") for the provision of services as defined in the associated Statement of Work or Service Agreement.

---

## 1. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Services"** | The specific services provided by KDCN as described in the Statement of Work. |
| **"Service Hours"** | The hours during which Services are available, as defined in the Statement of Work. |
| **"Response Time"** | The time taken by KDCN to respond to a service request after receiving it. |
| **"Resolution Time"** | The time taken by KDCN to resolve a service request and restore normal service. |
| **"Incident"** | Any event that disrupts or degrades the normal operation of Services. |
| **"Business Hours"** | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (EAT), excluding public holidays. |

---

## 2. Service Availability Commitment

KDCN is committed to providing a high level of service availability:

| Service Type | Target Availability |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Website / Public Services** | 99.9% uptime (excluding planned maintenance) |
| **Customer Support** | Business hours response |
| **Emergency Support** | 24/7 priority response |

---

## 3. Incident Classification

Incidents are classified based on their impact on services:

| Priority | Description | Response Time | Resolution Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P1 — Critical** | Service unavailable — complete outage | < 1 hour | < 4 hours |
| **P2 — High** | Service degraded — major impact | < 2 hours | < 8 hours |
| **P3 — Medium** | Service impaired — minor impact | < 4 hours | < 24 hours |
| **P4 — Low** | Service not impacted — inquiry only | < 8 hours | < 48 hours |

---

## 4. Service Level Monitoring

KDCN will monitor service levels and report on performance:
- Monthly service reports
- Quarterly business reviews
- Incident reports (available on request)

---

## 5. Client Responsibilities

To ensure effective service delivery, the Client agrees to:
- Provide accurate and timely information to KDCN.
- Assign a named point of contact for service-related matters.
- Report incidents promptly using the agreed reporting channels.
- Provide reasonable access to systems and infrastructure as needed.

---

## 6. Escalation Process

If a service issue is not resolved within the specified timeframe, it can be escalated:

| Level | Contact | Response Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Support Team | Immediate |
| **Level 2** | Support Manager | 30 minutes |
| **Level 3** | Director of Operations | 2 hours |

---

## 7. Exclusions

This SLA does not apply to:
- Issues caused by the Client's hardware, software, or third-party services.
- Planned maintenance scheduled with 48 hours' notice.
- Force majeure events beyond KDCN's control.

---

## 8. Reporting

KDCN will provide:
- **Monthly Service Report:** Performance against SLA targets.
- **Incident Report:** Details of any incidents that occurred during the month.

---

## 9. Review and Amendment

This SLA will be reviewed annually and may be amended by mutual agreement.
