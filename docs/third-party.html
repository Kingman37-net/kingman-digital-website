6. Data Processing Agreements

KDCN ensures that all third-party service providers process data in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including POPIA and GDPR. Where required, we have entered into Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with our service providers to:

  • Define the purposes and scope of data processing.
  • Ensure appropriate security measures are in place.
  • Outline data subject rights and breach notification procedures.
  • Restrict sub-processing and ensure compliance with applicable laws.

💡 Transparency Commitment: If we add new third-party service providers, we will update this page promptly. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties.
