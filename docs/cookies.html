1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They help the website remember your actions and preferences over time, improving your browsing experience.

Cookies are widely used to make websites function more efficiently and to provide information to the website owners.

Why Are Cookies Important?

  • Functionality: They enable basic functions like navigation and access to secure areas.
  • Performance: They help us understand how users interact with our site, allowing us to improve it.
  • Preferences: They remember your choices, such as dark/light mode or language preferences.
  • Security: They help protect against fraudulent activity and unauthorized access.

2. How We Use Cookies

KDCN uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Operations: To ensure the basic functionality of our website.
  • Performance Monitoring: To analyze how visitors use our site and improve user experience.
  • Preference Management: To remember your preferences and settings.
  • Security: To protect our website and users from security threats.
  • Compliance: To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies on our website:

  • Essential

    These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as navigation, security, and access to secure areas. You cannot opt out of these cookies as they are essential for the operation of the site.

  • Functional

    These cookies enhance your experience by remembering your preferences, such as theme selection (dark/light mode), language, and other settings.

  • Analytics

    These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data to improve our site's performance and user experience.

  • Marketing

    We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and seek your consent accordingly.
