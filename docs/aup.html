1. General Principles

Users of KDCN's website and services agree to:

  • Use the website and services lawfully and ethically.
  • Respect the security and integrity of our systems.
  • Not interfere with the ability of others to use the website.
  • Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.

2. Permitted Uses

You may use KDCN's website and services for:

  • Legitimate Research: Exploring cybersecurity, digital systems, and technology.
  • Educational Purposes: Learning about digital systems, security, and best practices.
  • Business Inquiries: Contacting KDCN for consultations, services, or partnerships.
  • Personal Learning: Accessing resources, guides, and documentation.
  • Professional Development: Enhancing skills through KDCN's educational content.

3. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on KDCN's website and services:

Illegal Activities

  • Using the website for any illegal purpose or in violation of any law.
  • Engaging in fraud, phishing, or identity theft.
  • Distributing child exploitation material or violent content.

Security Violations

  • Unauthorized Access: Attempting to gain unauthorized access to our systems, servers, or data.
  • Security Testing: Scanning, probing, or testing the vulnerability of our systems without explicit permission.
  • Malicious Code: Transmitting viruses, worms, Trojans, ransomware, or any harmful code.
  • Denial of Service: Attempting to disrupt or overload our systems.

Abusive Activities

  • Harassment: Abusing, threatening, or harassing KDCN staff, contractors, or other users.
  • Hate Speech: Transmitting content that promotes discrimination, hostility, or violence.
  • Misinformation: Deliberately spreading false or misleading information.

Intellectual Property Violations

  • Copyright Infringement: Reproducing, distributing, or modifying KDCN content without permission.
  • Trademark Infringement: Using KDCN's name, logo, or brand assets without authorization.
  • Scraping: Using automated systems to extract data from our website without permission.

Other Prohibited Activities

  • Spam: Sending unsolicited commercial messages through our systems.
  • Impersonation: Impersonating KDCN, its employees, or other users.
  • Misuse of Resources: Consuming excessive resources that affect other users' experience.
  • Reverse Engineering: Reverse engineering, decompiling, or disassembling any KDCN software or systems.

4. Security and Integrity

"No system left unsafe."

KDCN takes security seriously. By using our website, you agree to:

  • Protect any authentication credentials (usernames, passwords) provided to you.
  • Report any security vulnerabilities discovered to KDCN immediately.
  • Not exploit or disclose security issues without prior permission.
  • Not share your account credentials with others.

Violation of security policies may result in immediate termination of access and potential legal action.

5. Content Standards

Any content you upload, post, or transmit through KDCN's website must:

  • Be accurate and truthful to the best of your knowledge.
  • Not be misleading, deceptive, or fraudulent.
  • Not infringe on the rights of others.
  • Not contain inappropriate, offensive, or harmful material.
  • Not violate any applicable laws or regulations.

KDCN reserves the right to remove or reject any content that violates these standards.

6. Enforcement

KDCN reserves the right to:

  • Monitor: Monitor user activity on our website for compliance with this Policy.
  • Suspend: Suspend or terminate access to users who violate this Policy.
  • Investigate: Investigate suspected violations and cooperate with law enforcement.
  • Remove Content: Remove any content that violates this Policy.
  • Take Action: Take any appropriate action to protect KDCN's systems and users.

Serious violations may result in legal action, including reporting to relevant authorities.

7. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of any violation of this Acceptable Use Policy, please report it immediately to:

📧 kingmandigitalcybernetwork@gmail.com

Subject line: "AUP Violation Report"

Reports should include relevant information such as:

  • Description of the violation.
  • Location (URL, page, etc.) where the violation occurred.
  • Any supporting evidence (screenshots, timestamps).
  • Your contact information (optional).

8. Changes to This Policy

KDCN reserves the right to modify this Acceptable Use Policy at any time. We will notify users of significant changes through:

  • A notice on our website.
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Your continued use of the website after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.
