1. Our Security Commitment

Power without discipline is abuse. System without security is abuse.

KDCN exists to build digital infrastructure that organizations can depend on. Our security commitment is built on four core principles:

  • Discipline over Convenience: We prioritize security over shortcuts, ensuring that every system we build is hardened, documented, and maintainable.
  • Encryption Everywhere: All data in transit and at rest is encrypted using industry-standard protocols. We enforce HTTPS/TLS 1.3 across all services.
  • Zero-Trust Architecture: We verify everything, trust nothing. Every access request is authenticated, authorized, and encrypted.
  • Continuous Improvement: We regularly review and enhance our security practices to address evolving threats and emerging risks.

2. Security Standards & Controls

KDCN implements a comprehensive set of security controls across all layers of our operations:

Transport Layer Security

  • HTTPS / TLS 1.3: All communications between users and our website are encrypted using TLS 1.3, the current industry standard.
  • HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security): Enforced to prevent downgrade attacks and ensure all connections use HTTPS.
  • Certificate Management: We maintain valid SSL/TLS certificates and renew them before expiration.

Application Layer Security

  • CSP (Content Security Policy): We implement CSP headers to prevent XSS and other code injection attacks.
  • Input Validation: All user inputs are validated and sanitized to prevent injection attacks.
  • Safe JavaScript Practices: Our scripts follow secure coding practices to minimize vulnerabilities.
  • Dependency Management: We use Dependabot and regular reviews to keep all dependencies updated and secure.

Access Control

  • Multi-Factor Authentication (2FA): Enforced for all repository and system access.
  • Least Privilege Principle: Users and services are granted only the permissions they need.
  • Strong Credentials: All accounts use strong, unique passwords or passkeys.
  • Regular Audits: Access rights are reviewed regularly to ensure compliance.

Repository Security

  • Secret Protection: We use .gitignore and secret scanning to prevent accidental credential exposure.
  • Branch Protection: Our main branch (Root) is protected with required reviews and status checks.
  • Code Reviews: All changes undergo peer review before merging.
  • Dependency Review: We review all dependencies for known vulnerabilities before inclusion.

3. Data Protection

We handle all data with the highest level of care and protection. Our data protection practices include:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest where applicable.
  • Data Minimization: We collect only what is necessary and retain it only as long as required.
  • Access Controls: Data is accessible only to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • Secure Handling: We follow industry best practices for data handling and storage.
  • Compliance: We comply with POPIA, GDPR, and applicable data protection laws.

For details on how we handle personal data, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

4. Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

KDCN takes security seriously and welcomes responsible reporting of security vulnerabilities. We believe that open communication and collaboration with the security community help us maintain the highest standards of security.

This policy is designed to provide clear guidelines for security researchers who wish to report vulnerabilities they have discovered in our systems.

5. Responsible Disclosure Guidelines

What We Ask of You

  • Act in Good Faith: Report vulnerabilities in a responsible manner without exploiting them.
  • Provide Sufficient Details: Include clear steps to reproduce the issue, affected systems, and potential impact.
  • Be Patient: Allow us time to investigate and address the issue before disclosing it publicly.
  • Limit Testing: Only test systems that are explicitly within the scope of this policy.
  • Avoid Damage: Do not attempt to access or compromise data that does not belong to you.
  • Maintain Confidentiality: Do not share the vulnerability with others until we have had time to address it.

What We Promise You

  • Confidentiality: We will not share your personal information without your consent.
  • Acknowledgement: We will confirm receipt of your report and provide updates on its status.
  • Investigation: We will investigate all valid reports promptly and thoroughly.
  • Fix and Notification: We will work to fix the issue and notify you when it is resolved.
  • No Legal Action: We will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow these guidelines.

Out of Scope

The following are not currently eligible for vulnerability disclosure:

  • Denial of Service (DoS) attacks.
  • Physical security issues.
  • Social engineering or phishing attempts.
  • Issues that require access to physical infrastructure.
  • Issues that rely on outdated browsers or insecure client-side configurations.
  • Vulnerabilities in third-party services not operated by KDCN.

6. Reporting a Vulnerability

To report a security vulnerability, please contact us at:
📧 kingmandigitalcybernetwork@gmail.com
✉️ Subject line: "Security Vulnerability Report"

When submitting a report, please include the following information to help us investigate effectively:

  • A clear description of the vulnerability.
  • The affected component or URL.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
  • Potential impact or risk level.
  • Your contact information (name, email, and optionally, a PGP key for secure communication).
  • Any screenshots or proof-of-concept that may assist in understanding the issue.

We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 3 business days and provide a preliminary assessment.

7. What to Expect

When you submit a vulnerability report, here is what you can expect from us:

  • Day 1-3: Acknowledgment and initial assessment.
  • Week 1-2: Investigation and validation of the report.
  • Week 2-4: Development and testing of a fix.
  • Week 4-5: Deployment of the fix and communication of resolution.
  • After Resolution: We may request to add your name to our Acknowledgments page (with your consent).

Timelines may vary depending on the complexity of the issue. We will keep you informed throughout the process.

8. Our Security Pledge

"No data left unguarded.
No system left unsafe."

— This is not just our motto. It is our engineering standard.

KDCN is committed to continuous improvement in security. We:

  • Regularly review and update our security practices.
