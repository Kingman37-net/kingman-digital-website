## 📖 Overview

**KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK (KDCN)** is a **Kenya-based digital systems organization** headquartered in **Manyatta, Embu, Kenya**, with a global technology outlook.

KDCN is built around a clear engineering mandate: **to design, develop, and support secure, resilient, scalable, and sustainable digital systems.**

We do not build technology merely for appearance or short-term deployment. We engineer systems with **security, operational continuity, maintainability, and long-term growth** in mind.

---

## 🏛️ Architecture

KDCN uses a **three-layer conceptual model** to understand the modern digital environment:

| Layer | Percentage | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **🌐 Public Web** | **4%** | Websites, digital platforms, public interfaces, online services, and digital presence. |
| **🔧 Deep Web** | **90%** | Databases, APIs, authentication, infrastructure, internal systems, business processes, application services, and administrative systems. |
| **💀 Dark Web** | **6%** | Cybercrime, malicious infrastructure, unauthorized access, exploitation, and threat activity. |

> *These percentages represent KDCN's conceptual architectural model, not a statistical measurement of the internet.*

<div align="center">

**Our strategic position is clear:**

> *"Secure the systems beneath the surface so the visible digital layer can operate with confidence."*
