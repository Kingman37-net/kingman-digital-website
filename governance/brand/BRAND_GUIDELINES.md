# KDCN Brand Usage Guidelines

**KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK (KDCN)**

## 1. Brand Overview
KDCN stands for **KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK** — a Kenya-based digital systems organization headquartered in Manyatta, Embu, Kenya.

## 2. Brand Positioning
> *"No data left unguarded. No system left unsafe."*

## 3. Brand Promise
To design, develop, and support secure, resilient, scalable, and sustainable digital systems.

## 4. Logo Usage
The official KDCN logo consists of:
- **The Wolf** — symbolizing vigilance, guardianship, and strength.
- **The Monitor** — representing technology, systems, and digital infrastructure.
- **The Name** — "KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK" or "KDCN"

## 5. Brand Colors
| Color | Hex | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Dark Navy | `#0a0f14` | Backgrounds, text |
| Primary Blue | `#0b7fab` | Headers, buttons |
| Accent Cyan | `#00d1ff` | CTAs, highlights |
| White | `#ffffff` | Text on dark |

## 6. Typography
- **Primary Font:** Segoe UI
- **Fallback:** system-ui, sans-serif

## 7. Brand Voice
- Professional, confident, security-focused, forward-thinking.
