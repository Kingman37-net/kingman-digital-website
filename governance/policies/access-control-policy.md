# KDCN Access Control Policy

**Classification:** INTERNAL — SOURCE OF TRUTH

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how access to KDCN systems and data is granted, managed, and revoked.

## 2. Principles
- **Least Privilege:** Minimum access required.
- **Need-to-Know:** Access based on business need.
- **Regular Review:** Access rights reviewed quarterly.

## 3. Password Policy
- Minimum 12 characters
- Mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special characters
- Changed every 90 days

## 4. Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA required for all administrative accounts and GitHub access.

## 5. Access Review
- User Access Review: Quarterly
- Privileged Access Review: Monthly
