# KDCN Data Governance Policy

**Classification:** INTERNAL — SOURCE OF TRUTH

## 1. Data Ownership
Data generated by KDCN belongs to KDCN unless otherwise contractually agreed.

## 2. Data Classification
- Public
- Internal
- Confidential
- Restricted

## 3. Data Access
Access granted on a need-to-know basis with least privilege.

## 4. Data Retention
Data retained according to legal, regulatory, and business requirements.

## 5. Data Disposal
Data securely disposed of when no longer needed.
